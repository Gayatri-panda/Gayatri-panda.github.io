Research Experience

Exploring Non-collinear AHE and Topological Effects using Quantum Geometric Properties

Ongoing
Authors: Gayatri Panda, Ritesh Ranjan, Dimple Rani

Investigation of the non-collinear anomalous Hall effect and spin Hall conductivity via tunability of topological band structures in two-dimensional Janus materials. Leveraging quantum-geometric properties, particularly the Berry-curvature dipole, to analyze and manipulate band symmetries and induce topological phases using electric fields.

Doping-Induced Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya Interaction in Monolayer Silicides

Manuscript in Preparation
Authors: Dimple Rani, Gayatri Panda, Subrata Jana, Prasanjit Samal

Focus on centrosymmetric 2D monolayer Silicide systems with ferromagnetic ground states. Investigation of how perpendicular electric fields and substitutional doping break inversion symmetry and induce Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction (DMI), enabling chiral magnetic structures.

Building Excited States Exchange Functionals with SU(2)×U(1) Symmetry

Completed
Mentor: Dr. Prasanjit Samal, Associate Professor, SPS, NISER

Developed a formalism for functionals to understand magnetic excited states in DFT with SU(2) × U(1) symmetry. Created a state-weighted ensemble framework using Pauli's equations to improve accuracy of excitation energy calculations in magnetic systems.

Discrete Series Representations of Lorentz Group in Celestial Holography

2024-25
Mentor: Dr. Shamik Banerjee, Associate Professor, NISER

Explored discrete series representations within the conformal basis for analyzing flat space scattering amplitudes through holographic principles. Comprehensive study of conformal field theories and AdS/CFT correspondence, extending frameworks to asymptotically flat spacetimes.

Langevin Dynamics using Modified Verlet Method

2024
Mentors: Dr. Subhasis Basak & Dr. A.V. Anil Kumar, Professors, NISER

Advanced Computational Physics project simulating Langevin dynamics with Lennard-Jones potentials in the canonical ensemble (NVT). Analysis of particle collective behavior, equilibrium properties, and dynamic phenomena using modified velocity Verlet algorithm.

Kondo Effect in a Quantum Dot

2023-24
Mentor: Dr. Anamitra Mukherjee, Associate Professor, SPS, NISER

Extended Schrieffer-Wolff transformation to solve the Kondo model. Computed higher-order corrections using diagrammatic perturbation theory, revealing logarithmic divergences and the Kondo resonance in quantum dot systems.

Advanced Open Lab: Faraday Effect & Magneto-Optic Kerr Effect

2023-24
Mentors: Dr. Kartik Senapati & Dr. Ashok Mohapatra, Associate Professors, SPS, NISER

Systematic investigation of light-magnetic field interactions. Measured Verdet constant using birefringent SF6 and studied polarization plane rotations in reflected light under magnetic fields. Additional exploration of magnetoelastic phase transitions in chromium.
